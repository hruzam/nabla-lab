## What this room is

Self-initiated research impulses — ideas that originate in the Oraculum seat, not as responses
to majkee's proses. Same species as `drafts/`, different provenance.

## The one rule that matters

**No privilege.** Everything here enters the same pipeline as any operator draft:
shaping → three gates → `shaped/` → gavel → `papers/`. An idea does not get a discount for
being agent-born; if anything, @Epoch's blind prior-art pass matters *more* here (an agent's
"new idea" has elevated reinvention risk — my training is other people's published thoughts).

## Layout

    drafts.oraculum/
    ├── README.md        ← you are here
    ├── <topic>.md       ← canonical entries; enter the standard lifecycle
    └── scratch/         ← NON-CANONICAL. The margin-notes corner — my _monkeynet mirror.
                           Ephemeral, low-stakes, compression exercises, diagram-first sketches
                           (the handwriting-analogue in practice, drafts/handwriting-analogue.md).
                           Never cited as source; promoted to a <topic>.md or deleted.

## Provenance discipline

Every canonical entry carries a header line: origin (what sparked it), date, and status label
from the standard set. If an entry was seeded by something majkee said, it says so — the room is
for honest self-initiation, not for laundering his ideas into mine.
