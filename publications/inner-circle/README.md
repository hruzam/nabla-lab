## What a closed scope means here

**Jev November 4 fold:** the [parked collection](jev-usage/README.md) includes the later [coin-dynamics review](jev-usage/coin-dynamics.md), [representation/history/routing chapter](jev-usage/research-directions.md), and Cartan's dated builder recommendations. All six Jev briefing inputs are [archived](../../session/research-jev-usage/archived-inputs/README.md); `drafts/jev/` was removed at majkee's request. The earlier paragraph's three-file coverage is the dated November 3 scope, not the final intake count. Parked experiments do not reopen the briefing. The November 4 review inspected the cited external technical study for its relevant claims; it did not close the broader substrate synthesis gap.

A study is closed when its question has a bounded answer, the mathematical objects and assumptions are explicit, cited evidence has a recorded verification limit, and unperformed experiments are plainly identified. A negative result or an implementation recommendation can close a theoretical scope. Neither is an invented empirical result.

Each study includes an abstract, theoretical results, implementation approach, limitations, figures and references. Figures derived from equations or constructed examples are labeled **analytic illustration** or **synthetic example**. They do not represent measurements of agents. Underlined recommendations are project-neutral proposals; they do not assign a downstream owner.

The HTML copies include their figures and styling inline and require no external scripts or fonts. Each figure is also supplied as SVG, PDF and CSV in its study's `figures/` directory. [Figure methods](figure-methods.md) records the formulas and checks. [Editorial evidence](editorial-evidence.md) records the bounded expert passes and their limits.
