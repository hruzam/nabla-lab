## Reuse rules

- Use Jev for bounded judgments over inspectable evidence; compare against the strongest feasible simple method.
- Keep measurement, interpretation, composition, observation selection and action policy distinguishable.
- A richer representation can preserve information; it cannot conjure an unobserved cause. A confidence statistic does not establish physical edge orientation or a new ontology.
- The coin's geometry, its dynamics, categorical uncertainty and operational session states are distinct models. Any bridge between them needs an explicit map and a test.
- Established mechanisms, direct derivations, synthetic checks and proposed experiments have different evidence status. No live Jev benchmark, cross-vendor validation or new canon is claimed here.
