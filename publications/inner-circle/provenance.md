## Citation and closure cautions

Bare references such as `cross-measure.2026-07-02.md` resolve to the research directory above. They are abbreviated paths, not evidence of missing research. Likewise “follow-up B” is a section of the named follow-up, not an absent file that should be invented.

The Atlas №2 publication date for the generalized Kuramoto article has two legitimate stages: online 2024-10-02; journal issue January 2025. The relative-representations reference needs a real bibliographic correction: Moschella et al.'s ICLR paper is arXiv:2209.15430; arXiv:2303.00721 is Cannistraci et al.'s bootstrapping follow-up.

The new studies distinguish three things: SHIFT_1's historical findings, corrections established in this reconstruction, and proposed future tests. A later correction does not erase the fact that research or release occurred.
